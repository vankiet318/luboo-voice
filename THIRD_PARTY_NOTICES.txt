Luboo — Voice Assistant
========================

Luboo is free software for personal, non-commercial use.

By installing, you agree that:
- Your voice is recorded only after a wake word is detected (or you press the
  microphone button), and is sent to Groq (https://groq.com) for speech recognition
  and to generate answers.
- Replies are read aloud using Microsoft Edge's online text-to-speech service.
- Your Groq API key is stored only on this computer and is sent only to Groq.


Third-party components
----------------------

Wake word models "Hey Luboo" and "Luboo ơi" (hey_luboo, luboo_oi) — trained for Luboo.
  Training used openWakeWord's pre-computed ACAV100M features and validation set,
  MIT environmental impulse responses, and synthetic speech from Piper voices and edge-tts.
